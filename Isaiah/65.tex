  \pb{}The past things shall neither be remembered\pa{}nor be elevated in the heart.\alt{come to mind.}%
  \ed{So why should I remember or rejoice in it (the world) now?}%%

  \pb{}\vrs{65:18} However, be joyous and rejoice \textit{ad infinitum}\pa{}that I am the Creator!%%

  \pb{}For behold, I create for Jerusalem a rejoicing\pa{}and a joy for its people.%%

  \pb{}\vrs{65:19} I have rejoiced in Jerusalem\pa{}and have joy in My people.%%

  \pb{}The voice\alt{sound} of weeping is not heard in her,\pa{}neither is the voice of crying heard.%%

  \pb{}\vrs{65:20} There shall not come from thence\pa{}an infant of days,%%

  \pb{}\vrs{65:22} They shall not build and have another inhabit;\pa{}they shall not plant and have another eat thereof.%%

  \pb{}For the days of My people shall be as the days of a tree;\pa{}My chosen shall long enjoy the work of their hands.%%

  \pb{}\vrs{65:23} They shall not labor in vain,\pa{}nor bring forth for terror.\alt{calamity.}%%
